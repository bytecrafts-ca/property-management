# After launch: how to push for #1 in Durham

The website is only half the fight. Local #1 is mostly Google Business Profile + reviews + consistency.

## Do this week (highest impact)

1. **Push/deploy this site** when you are ready (say the word). Until then Google still sees the old site.
2. **Google Business Profile**
   - Claim/optimize 9th Star Property Management
   - Category: Property management company
   - Service areas: Pickering, Ajax, Whitby, Oshawa, Clarington, Bowmanville, Courtice, Brooklin, Newcastle, Scugog, Uxbridge
   - Phone: 416-834-3587
   - Email: info@9thstarpropertymanagement.ca
   - Website: https://9thstarpropertymanagement.ca
   - Description with Durham + city names written naturally
   - Add services matching the site
   - Upload real photos
3. **Google Search Console**
   - Verify the domain
   - Submit `https://9thstarpropertymanagement.ca/sitemap.xml`
4. **Ask for Google reviews** from happy landlords (real only). Aim for steady new reviews, not a fake burst.
5. **Citations** with identical NAP: Yellow Pages, Homestars, Yelp, Nextdoor, local chambers where eligible.

## Weeks 2–8

- 1 GBP post per week (tips, city notes, vacancies, review asks)
- Reply to every review
- Publish or refresh 1 guide/month once the core pages are indexed
- Check Search Console weekly for queries and pages getting impressions

## Weeks 8–12

- Double down on city pages that already show impressions
- Get 2–3 local partner mentions (HVAC, cleaning, realtor) linking to your site
- Consider light Google Ads on “property management Pickering/Ajax/Whitby” while organic builds

## What not to do

- Do not buy reviews
- Do not stuff keywords into GBP
- Do not use a different phone number on directories than on the site/GBP
- Do not expect #1 in 7 days. Local winners usually take weeks to months with consistent GBP + reviews.
